# Summary

Screens for Parkmed's internal apps (Statutory Hub, EmployRP, PO & Invoices, SupplyRP). They are
built in Oracle APEX afterwards, so every screen here is a specification for a real page.

- Who it is for: Parkmed staff at work (Accounts, HR, Operations, Procurement, Compliance) and
  the COO and CEO, who approve. They use the apps all day on laptops, and on phones to approve.
- What it should make them do: find the record, do the next step of their own process, and see
  what needs them. Nothing decorative.
- The feeling it should leave: calm, dense, exact. A ledger, not a landing page.
